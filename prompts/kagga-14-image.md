# Kagga 14 comic image prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: final landscape six-panel comic for the Kagga Bites literary blog

Use the established Mankuthimma character reference and the collection's expressive Kagga 241 strip only for recurring-character identity and general collection continuity. Preserve the ordinary middle-aged Kannada householder with untidy dark hair touched with gray, modest moustache, weathered kindly face, off-white cotton shirt, white panche/dhoti with muted blue edge, faded rust shoulder cloth, worn sandals, and blue-gray cloth bag. Keep every new scene and composition original.

Create a six-panel interpretation of Kagga 14: every human lives beneath one sky, treads one earth, eats grain, drinks water, and breathes air in common, yet hostility divides them. Use a lively Karnataka village and market in sky blue, field gold, terracotta, and leafy green. Avoid simplistic social or ethnic caricatures.

Use exactly six equal rectangular pictures in a clean 3x2 grid with narrow warm-paper gutters and one cream caption band at the bottom of every picture:

1. A varied village and market beneath one continuous generous sky; Mankuthimma looks upward. Caption: “One generous sky covered every village roof.”
2. A low energetic view of varied feet, sandals, bare feet, a cartwheel, and Mankuthimma all pressing the same ochre earth. Caption: “Every hurried foot pressed the same earth.”
3. Diverse neighbors harvest and pass sheaves from one continuous golden field while wary glances begin. Caption: “Different hands gathered grain from one field.”
4. Signature image: two stubborn quarreling neighbors face away while drinking from cups filled from the same large clay water pot; Mankuthimma steadies it between them. Caption: “Quarreling neighbors drank from one clay pot.”
5. One playful gust lifts both rivals' turbans into the same curling current; both scramble with comically identical expressions. Caption: “One breeze lifted both their angry turbans.”
6. The rivals and Mankuthimma pause beneath open sky as hostility softens without being magically resolved. Caption: “Who taught shared breath to become hatred?”

Use an original lively hand-drawn Sunday-newspaper adventure-comic language in watercolor and ink: variable-width brush-and-pen contours, gestural marks, expressive mature acting, visual wit, transparent pigment, visible paper grain, soft blooms, dry-brush accents, white-paper highlights, bold staging, and generous breathing room. Mankuthimma is never a child, monk, guru, priest, professor, superhero, or renunciate.

Critical constraint: exactly six pictures and exactly one supplied English narration caption per picture. Render all six captions verbatim, in reading order, using large dark legible hand-lettering. No Kannada script, dialogue, speech bubbles, title, labels, panel numbers, logo, watermark, credits, footer, signage, pseudo-writing, or any other text. Do not imitate any named artist or reproduce recognizable copyrighted comic characters, designs, motifs, or compositions.
