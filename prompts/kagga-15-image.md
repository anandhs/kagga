# Kagga 15 comic image prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: final landscape six-panel comic for the Kagga Bites literary blog

Use the established Mankuthimma character reference and the collection's expressive Kagga 241 strip only for recurring-character identity and general collection continuity. Preserve the ordinary middle-aged Kannada householder with untidy dark hair touched with gray, modest moustache, weathered kindly face, off-white cotton shirt, white panche/dhoti with muted blue edge, faded rust shoulder cloth, worn sandals, and blue-gray cloth bag. Keep every new scene and composition original.

Create a six-panel interpretation of Kagga 15: inherited devotion and trust have faded, but no bright new worldview has arrived, leaving society anxious between shelters. Treat this as cultural and existential transition, not propaganda for or against religion. Use no sectarian symbols. Shift from worn amber and rust to misty blue-gray, ending with the faintest possible dawn.

Use exactly six equal rectangular pictures in a clean 3x2 grid with narrow warm-paper gutters and one cream caption band at the bottom of every picture:

1. Inside a familiar but aging ordinary home, plain oil lamps dim while Mankuthimma notices. No altar or religious icon. Caption: “Old lamps dimmed inside the familiar house.”
2. Trusted rafters crack and fall as Mankuthimma and neighbors escape safely, with dynamic dust and movement but no injury. Caption: “One morning, its trusted rafters finally fell.”
3. Mankuthimma stumbles through dust, clutching an extinguished cold lantern from the old home; shaken, not slapstick. Caption: “He stumbled outward, clutching yesterday’s cold lantern.”
4. He raises the lantern into unsettled fog; no new beacon or certain path appears, only displaced neighbors. Caption: “No new beacon pierced the unsettled fog.”
5. Signature image: Mankuthimma stands centered between the rubble of the collapsed familiar house and the bare foundations and timber frame of a possible new house. Caption: “Behind lay rubble; ahead, unfinished foundations.”
6. An ordinary community waits together between rubble and unfinished frame; a sparrow rests on the first new beam and fog barely warms. Caption: “The world waited, homeless between its certainties.”

Use an original lively hand-drawn Sunday-newspaper adventure-comic language in watercolor and ink: variable-width brush-and-pen contours, gestural marks, expressive mature acting, transparent pigment, visible paper grain, soft blooms, dry-brush accents, white-paper highlights, bold staging, and generous breathing room. Mankuthimma is never a child, monk, guru, priest, professor, superhero, or renunciate.

Critical constraint: exactly six pictures and exactly one supplied English narration caption per picture. Render all six captions verbatim, in reading order, using large dark legible hand-lettering. No Kannada script, dialogue, speech bubbles, title, labels, panel numbers, logo, watermark, credits, footer, signage, pseudo-writing, or any other text. Do not imitate any named artist or reproduce recognizable copyrighted comic characters, designs, motifs, or compositions.
