# Kagga 750 comic image prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: final landscape six-panel comic for the Kagga Bites literary blog

Use the established Mankuthimma character reference and the collection's expressive Kagga 241 strip as identity and art-direction references. Preserve the same ordinary middle-aged Kannada householder: wavy untidy dark hair touched with gray, modest moustache, weathered kindly face, attentive eyes, subtle age lines, handwoven off-white cotton shirt, white traditional panche/dhoti with muted blue edge, faded rust shoulder cloth, worn sandals on shore, and practical blue-gray cloth bag.

Create a completely original six-panel visual interpretation of Kagga 750 about engaged detachment: stay afloat in life's current, do good, give and receive joy, meet the final call without panic, and remain joined to the world. Do not reduce it to dropping all responsibilities, emotional numbness, solitary escape, or carefree positivity.

Use a broad welcoming Karnataka river during an ordinary communal crossing, moving from bright morning through golden dusk. The water grows from knee-deep at the bank to a strong but non-catastrophic midstream current, then widens peacefully toward the far shore. People swim, wade, steady small woven floats, and help one another. Use exactly six equal rectangular pictures on one wide landscape page, in a clean three-column by two-row grid with narrow warm-paper gutters. Place exactly one cream caption band at the bottom of every picture.

1. At bright morning, Mankuthimma enters the broad river with diverse ordinary travelers. The current tugs at everyone's legs and clothes; he leans into it with surprised concentration. His sandals are tied to his bag. Tiny sparrow on a riverside reed. Caption: “Life’s broad river pulled at every traveler.”
2. Midstream, he clutches his soaked cloth bag plus two unnecessary bundles high against his chest. Their weight drags him comically low until only his alarmed face and moustache remain above the lively water. Nearby travelers notice and reach toward him. Caption: “Clinging to every burden dragged him beneath.”
3. He lets the two extra bundles settle safely onto a small nearby woven float, loosens his panicked grip, and rises back to the surface with a great breath and relieved expression. Caption: “He loosened his grip and reached the surface.”
4. A clear chest-deep view at the sunlit waterline. Mankuthimma smiles as one open hand gives a ripe guava to a child on a woven float while his other open hand accepts a braided rescue rope from an older woman. Their hands form one flowing diagonal across the sparkling current. Make this exchange the unforgettable focal image. Caption: “Joy traveled both ways through open hands.”
5. At golden dusk, the far-bank ferryman raises one small amber lantern and rings a simple hand bell, calling the remaining travelers ashore. Mankuthimma hears it, turns calmly, and releases the last unnecessary bundle toward the shared float. No fear, funeral, or supernatural imagery. Caption: “At dusk, the farther shore quietly called.”
6. In peach-lavender twilight, Mankuthimma moves calmly with the other travelers toward the open far bank and its welcoming lantern. He remains within the human current rather than posing as a lone hero. The river widens into luminous distance, and the sparrow flies with the group. Caption: “Untroubled, he moved onward within the whole.”

Use an original, exuberant hand-drawn Sunday newspaper adventure-comic language in watercolor and ink: lively variable-width black brush-and-pen contours, swift gestural marks, expressive silhouettes, elastic but clearly middle-aged facial acting, playful physical comedy in panel 2, tender human interaction, transparent pigment washes, visible paper grain, soft blooms, dry-brush accents, lively splashes, and white-paper highlights. Use clear bold staging, immediate readability, generous breathing room, and warm handcrafted print character. Palette: soft river blue and turquoise, leafy green, warm sienna banks, off-white cotton, muted indigo, faded rust, guava green, amber lantern, peach-gold dusk, and lavender distance.

Mankuthimma is an adult Kannada householder and participant in ordinary life, never a monk, guru, renunciate, pilgrim, priest, superhero, professor, child, or all-knowing sage. Keep compact mature adult proportions and consistent identity. Do not imitate any named artist or include any recognizable copyrighted comic character, child protagonist, tiger, stuffed animal, signature motif, or specific published-comic composition.

Critical constraint: exactly six pictures and exactly one supplied English narration caption per picture. Render the six quoted captions verbatim, in reading order, using large dark highly legible hand-lettering. A straight apostrophe in “Life's” is acceptable, but no other text changes are allowed. Do not paraphrase, misspell, truncate, duplicate, or omit any word. No Kannada script, dialogue, speech bubbles, title, labels, panel numbers, logo, watermark, credits, footer, signage, decorative writing, pseudo-writing, or other text anywhere in the image.
