# Kagga 27 comic image prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: final landscape six-panel comic for the Kagga Bites literary blog

Use the established Mankuthimma character reference and the collection's expressive Kagga 241 strip only for recurring-character identity and general collection continuity. Preserve the ordinary middle-aged Kannada householder with untidy dark hair touched with gray, modest moustache, weathered kindly face, off-white cotton shirt, white panche/dhoti with muted blue edge, faded rust shoulder cloth, worn sandals, and blue-gray cloth bag. Keep every new scene and composition original.

Create a six-panel interpretation of Kagga 27: what does human life achieve if all its struggle and circling amount only to filling the stomach? Preserve DVG's open question. Do not preach, glorify poverty, blame people struggling to survive, or demean animals. Let a bustling Karnataka market gradually reveal itself as a circular track, moving from morning gold to tired violet dusk.

Use exactly six equal rectangular pictures in a clean 3x2 grid with narrow warm-paper gutters and one cream caption band at the bottom of every picture:

1. A morning market opens as Mankuthimma, animals, birds, merchants, and families begin searching for food. Caption: “Morning sent everyone searching for their meal.”
2. An overhead playful view shows hooves, wings, cartwheels, and human footsteps tracing circles around grain stalls. Caption: “Hooves, wings, and footsteps circled the market.”
3. Mankuthimma races with strained determination, protecting a small gathered meal bowl while the crowd loops behind him. Caption: “He raced harder, guarding each gathered morsel.”
4. At sunset, cattle, a dog, and birds rest peacefully after eating while Mankuthimma keeps hurrying. Caption: “By dusk, the animals rested after eating.”
5. Signature image: a worn circular footpath surrounds the same grain stall; resting animals sit outside while exhausted Mankuthimma keeps circling with tomorrow's empty bowl. Caption: “Still he circled, clutching tomorrow’s empty bowl.”
6. At twilight he finally stops on the circular track and looks back at his own loop, bowl lowered, with open distance beyond. Caption: “Had all that striving carried him farther?”

Use an original lively hand-drawn Sunday-newspaper adventure-comic language in watercolor and ink: variable-width brush-and-pen contours, gestural marks, expressive mature acting, playful movement, transparent pigment, visible paper grain, soft blooms, dry-brush accents, white-paper highlights, bold staging, and generous breathing room. Mankuthimma is never a child, monk, guru, priest, professor, superhero, or renunciate.

Critical constraint: exactly six pictures and exactly one supplied English narration caption per picture. Render all six captions verbatim, in reading order, using large dark legible hand-lettering. No Kannada script, dialogue, speech bubbles, title, labels, panel numbers, logo, watermark, credits, footer, signage, pseudo-writing, or any other text. Do not imitate any named artist or reproduce recognizable copyrighted comic characters, designs, motifs, or compositions.
