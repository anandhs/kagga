# Kagga 762 comic image prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: final landscape comic strip for the Kagga Bites literary blog

Create one coherent six-panel watercolor-and-ink comic interpreting Kagga 762 as the strength supplied by remembering a high orienting goal and a distant true home. Remembrance does not erase distance, pain, or bodily limitation, and the traveler does not triumphantly arrive.

Use the supplied Kagga 2 comic strictly as the recurring wayfarer identity and art-direction reference. Preserve the same mature Kannada man: weathered attentive face, graying curly untidy dark hair, modest moustache, off-white cotton shirt, white panche/dhoti with muted border, faded rust shoulder cloth, indigo cloth travel bag, worn sandals, and mature adult proportions. He is an ordinary wayfarer between destinations, neither settled householder nor monk, pilgrim, priest, guru, or renunciate. He can become distracted, walk in circles, suffer a painful foot, remember, and continue one step. Avoid heroic overcoming and disability-as-inspiration imagery.

Use exactly six equal undivided pictures on one wide landscape page, in a clean three-column by two-row grid with narrow warm-paper gutters. Do not use mini-panels, split scenes, inset frames, memory bubbles, or montage divisions. Reading order is left to right across the top row, then the bottom row. Place exactly one warm-cream caption band at the bottom of each picture with large dark legible English lettering.

The continuous geography is a Karnataka lowland road and town beneath one immense distant blue mountain, Meru. A tiny mountain village with one distinctive warm square window sits high along the route. Keep the mountain silhouette, stone town fountain, wayfarer, clothes, bag, and later foot bandage consistent.

1. At clear morning, the wayfarer stands on an open earth road looking toward the immense blue mountain and tiny village far along its slope. The road begins toward it. His purpose is humble, not heroic. Caption: “He began his journey toward the high mountain.”
2. In a busy lowland fair at midday, cloth, food, music, carts, and ordinary activity draw his gaze. He turns aside with the crowd and the mountain slips behind awnings. Include no signs or writing. Caption: “Amid the busy fair, he forgot his purpose.”
3. At late afternoon, he reaches the same recognizable stone fountain for a second time after descending through looping town lanes. He notices his own damp footprint and the familiar sparrow and realizes he circled back. Keep one continuous scene rather than a map or montage. Caption: “Without that memory, his path circled downward.”
4. Beyond town at dusk, he sits on a roadside stone and carefully wraps one sore, blistered foot in plain clean cloth. Show fatigue and discomfort while the mountain remains very far. Treat this as an ordinary travel injury, never a moral defect, miracle, or spectacle. Caption: “The mountain stayed distant, and his foot ached.”
5. At blue twilight, use a low viewpoint beside the plainly bandaged foot in the foreground. Far beyond, enormous blue Meru rises and one warm square window glows in the tiny mountain village. He recognizes that distant home and looks quietly. Keep the foot, mountain, and window unmistakably clear, without mystical rays or a vision bubble. Caption: “Then one warm window reminded him of home.”
6. At early dawn, he rises carefully and takes one modest step toward the mountain village without a staff. His travel bag remains on his shoulder, the mountain is still far, and he has not arrived. He is tired but reoriented, not victorious. Caption: “That remembered home gave strength for another step.”

Use warm reflective hand-painted watercolor, loose uneven ink, transparent washes, visible paper grain, soft blooms, mature anatomy, and generous breathing room. Palette: off-white, faded rust, muted indigo, earth brown, restrained fair colors, distant mountain blue, twilight violet, one amber window, and pale peach dawn. Keep the mountain visually constant while the wayfarer's attention to it changes.

Critical constraint: exactly six pictures and exactly one supplied English narration caption per picture. Render all six captions verbatim, in order, in consistent caption bands. Do not paraphrase, misspell, duplicate, or omit them. No Kannada, pseudo-writing, dialogue, speech bubbles, labels, title, panel numbers, logo, watermark, credits, footer, signs, decorative script, parchment, or other text.

Keep the exact same wayfarer identity in every panel. No saffron or orange robe, shaven head, bald monk, religious mark, halo, prayer beads, religious staff, begging bowl, clerical clothing, guru pose, serene omniscience, miraculous healing, oversized head, or childlike features.
