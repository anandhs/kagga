## English translation

*Working translation by Kagga Bites, informed by the sources below.*

What are earthly life's aim and fruit?  
Mere detours, struggle, and wandering?  
Beyond beasts and birds that roam to fill their bellies,  
what does a human accomplish, Mankutimma?

## English explanation and summary

Kagga 27 asks whether motion is the same as purpose. Earthly life appears full of detours, collisions, struggle, and repeated circulation. Animals and birds move in search of food; if human effort ends at the same necessity, DVG asks what its celebrated achievements amount to.

The comparison does not demean animals, food, or the labor needed to survive. Nor should it be used to judge those whose circumstances leave little room beyond necessity. Its challenge falls most sharply on self-important busyness: activity that calls itself progress while returning to the same starting point.

The verse deliberately withholds an answer. In a culture of constant work, consumption, and preparation for tomorrow, the worn market loop is still recognizable. The question is not whether we moved quickly, but whether anything in our striving carried life beyond appetite alone.
