## The idea

*Mankuthimmana Kagga* holds observation, doubt, humor, endurance, and wonder in compact four-line verses. Kagga Bites gives each selected verse one memorable visual metaphor and a six-panel watercolor story.

The comic is not a replacement for the Kagga. It is a doorway back to the original Kannada.

## How each comic is made

Every piece begins by verifying the original verse, translation, and available Kannada commentary. The interpretation is then distilled into one signature image before a storyboard is written. Only storyboards that pass a faithfulness, clarity, emotion, memorability, and simplicity review move into illustration.

The small orange-robed traveler who appears throughout the collection is not DVG or a religious teacher. He is a quiet observer—a place for the reader inside the frame.

## A note on interpretation

Kagga admits more than one reading. Each comic presents an interpretation, not a final explanation. Where sources disagree, the original Kannada remains authoritative and the ambiguity belongs to the poem.
